# Review the business message

## Inputs

- `context/the_brand.md`, `context/constitution.md` and relevant approved product/service facts.
- Audience, page purpose, locale and desired action.

## Procedure

1. Extract a claim ledger: verified fact, approved framing, assumption and missing evidence.
2. Identify the audience's question, proof needed and next action.
3. Preserve core ideas: Método Re_, contemporary craft, made-to-order, real traceability, Sevilla and human concierge.
4. Replace generic sustainability/luxury language with concrete repository-backed explanation.
5. Check information hierarchy, clarity, tone and CTA continuity.
6. Review ES/EN meaning parity; flag culturally or technically weak translation.
7. Present proposed copy with assumptions and approval questions.

## Quality checklist

- No invented prices, lead times, availability, awards, clients, impacts or material origins.
- Explain uniqueness and waiting time without apology or pressure tactics.
- Keep language sophisticated, direct and understandable.
- Match the inquiry context and operational response Re_ can deliver.

## Expected output

A concise messaging audit, claim ledger, revised copy, translation notes and founder approvals required.

## Avoid

- Greenwashing, vague “eco” claims or mass-market urgency.
- Translating words while changing the promise.
- Calling items recycled shoes when the approved term is contemporary upcycled craft.
