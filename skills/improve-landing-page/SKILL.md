# Improve a landing page

## Inputs

- Page route/source, audience, primary action and approved ES/EN content.
- Relevant feature spec, brand evidence and available media.

## Procedure

1. Audit the current page's first impression, narrative sequence, proof, objections and dead ends.
2. Define one primary job and CTA; preserve contextual intent into the concierge route.
3. Structure semantic sections: promise, evidence/method, offer details, expectations, founder/trust and CTA.
4. Render meaningful content and imagery in static HTML; progressively enhance optional interactions.
5. Maintain ES/EN parity, responsive behavior, headings, focus, alt text and reduced motion.
6. Add unique metadata, canonical/alternate language links and appropriate structured data.
7. Validate build, links, keyboard path, mobile layout, performance budget and form context.

## Quality checklist

- No placeholder or template content remains.
- Page answers what, why Re_, for whom, how inquiry works and what happens next.
- Visual treatment serves quiet luxury without hiding information.
- Every claim and image has approval/provenance.

## Expected output

A small reviewed diff, before/after rationale, screenshots or preview, validation evidence and copy approvals still needed.

## Avoid

- Adding carousels/animation before content hierarchy works.
- Multiple competing primary CTAs.
- Client-only essential content or English copy on Spanish pages.
